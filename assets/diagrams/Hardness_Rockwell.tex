Step 1, Preload: downward force F0 pushes the indenter to an initial dashed reference depth. Step 2, Preload plus Major Load: a larger downward force F0 plus F1 drives the indenter to a deeper dashed full-load depth. Step 3, Dwell: a leader between the second and third plates indicates the dwell period under major load. Step 4, Rebound: removing the major load while maintaining F0 allows the indenter to rise to an intermediate settled depth, with dimension arrows marking the permanent indentation depth h between the initial preload line and the final rebound line.}
